# Community code of conduct

Meteo welcomes technical disagreement and direct criticism. It does not accept harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption or publication of private location and security information.

Participants must:

- discuss work and evidence instead of attacking people;
- respect accessibility needs and different experience levels;
- disclose conflicts of interest relevant to a proposal;
- avoid deceptive claims about provider accuracy or official alerts;
- follow maintainer instructions when containing a security incident.

Maintainers may edit, reject or remove contributions and restrict participation when conduct threatens people, project safety or productive collaboration. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate and documented privately.

Report conduct incidents to `sylithtech.contact@gmail.com` with the subject `Meteo conduct report`. Do not include sensitive evidence in public issues.
