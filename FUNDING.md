# Funding policy

Meteo is free software and remains functionally identical for every user. It contains no advertising, subscriptions, paid features, paid early access, donation prompts or payment processing.

## What voluntary support may fund

External voluntary support may fund general Sylith project expenses such as development time, accessibility testing, hardware, provider infrastructure, domain names, release signing and documentation.

Financial support does **not** purchase:

- exclusive or early Meteo versions;
- additional weather data or features;
- priority security fixes or bug handling;
- control over roadmap or technical decisions;
- removal of GPL obligations;
- official endorsement by Sylith.

## Channel policy

No funding account is published until its holder, payout details and tax treatment are verified. When enabled, the preferred order is:

1. **[GitHub Sponsors](https://docs.github.com/sponsors)** for transparent one-time or recurring support tied to the maintainer and repository.
2. **[Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/)** for simple one-time external contributions.
3. **[PayPal.Me](https://www.paypal.com/paypalme/)** only as a payment fallback.

[Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/) is not planned because membership tiers create unnecessary expectations of exclusive content. [Kickstarter](https://www.kickstarter.com/) is unsuitable for ongoing maintenance and is not currently available directly to Chile-based project creators.

Funding links may appear on the Sylith website, GitHub profile or repository. They must not be embedded in Meteo while the current non-commercial provider arrangement is in use.

## Current legal status

Sylith Technologies is an independent project name, not an incorporated company or registered charity. Until that changes, support is received by the identified individual account holder and must not be described as a tax-deductible charitable donation.

Questions: `sylithtech.contact@gmail.com`.
